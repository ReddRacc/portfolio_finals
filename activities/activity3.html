How to make Lumpia

Lumpia Ingredients

  • 50 Pcs Lumpia Wrapper
  • 3 Cups Cooking Oil

Lumpia Filling Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lbs Ground Pork
  • 2 pcs Onion (minced)
  • 2 pcs Carrots (minced)
  • 1 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Ground Black Pepper
  • 1/2 cup Parsley (chopped)
  • 1 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 1 tbsp Sesami Oil
  • 2 Eggs

Instructions to Cooking Lumpia

  1. Combine all filling Ingredients in a bowl
  2. Scoop around 1 to 1 1/2 tablespoons of filling and place over a piece of lumpia wrapper.
    Spread the filling and then fold both sides of the wrapper.
    Fold the bottom. Brush beaten egg mixture on the top end of the wrapper.
    Roll-up until completely wrapped. Perform the same step until all mixture are consumed.
  3. Heat oil in a cooking pot. Deep fry lumpia in medium heat until it floats.
  4. Remove from the pot. Let excess oil drip. Serve. Share and enjoy
